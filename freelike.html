Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful notes before you place an order.

How do I check the price of a Rare ID?

Open the Rare IDs section, find the ID, and tap its WhatsApp order button. The store will confirm the current price and availability.

Are all listings always available?

Not necessarily. Listings and availability can change. Please confirm with UDITH FF STORE before making a decision.

Where do I get payment details?

Choose EZ Cash, Bank Transfer or Binance in Payment Methods and request the correct details directly from the store.

How can I follow store announcements?

Use the Join WhatsApp Channel button to open the official channel link supplied by the store.

Can I ask about a service before ordering?

Yes. Use any service card's WhatsApp button to ask for details, availability and final pricing.

Does this website process payments?

No. This is a catalogue and contact website. Confirm details directly with the store and only pay after verifying the recipient.
